When writing captions:

## Hook first (most important rule)
The first line must stop the scroll. Options:
- A bold statement: "Most people get this completely wrong."
- A question: "What would you do with an extra hour every day?"
- A relatable truth: "Nobody talks about the messy middle."
- A surprising fact or number: "93% of communication is non-verbal."

## Caption structure
1. **Hook** (1 line) — stop the scroll
2. **Body** (3–6 lines) — expand the idea, tell the story, share the value
3. **CTA** (1 line) — tell them what to do next

## Call-to-action options (rotate, don't repeat the same one)
- "Save this for later."
- "Tag someone who needs to hear this."
- "Drop a emoji in the comments if you agree."
- "Share this with your team."
- "Follow for more tips like this."
- "What's your experience? Tell me below."

## Tone guide by content type
| Visual type | Tone |
|-------------|------|
| Product photo | Aspirational, benefit-focused |
| Behind the scenes | Casual, personal, authentic |
| Motivational quote | Inspiring, punchy |
| Tutorial / how-to | Clear, instructional |
| Lifestyle / personal | Warm, relatable, story-driven |
| Promotional | Confident, direct, urgency-driven |

## Rules
- Keep paragraphs to 1–2 lines for mobile readability
- No emojis unless the user's brand uses them
- Ask about tone or brand voice if not clear
- Output only the caption — no labels or extra commentary
