When writing social media posts, follow these platform-specific rules:

## Twitter / X
- Maximum 280 characters — count carefully
- Lead with a strong hook (question, bold claim, or surprising fact)
- Use 1–3 inline hashtags naturally woven into the text
- One clear idea per tweet; no filler
- End with a call-to-action or open question when appropriate

## LinkedIn
- Length: 150–300 words
- Open with a single powerful sentence (no "I am excited to share" openers)
- Use short paragraphs (1–3 lines max) for mobile readability
- Tell a story or share a concrete insight
- Close with a question to drive comments
- Professional but human tone — avoid corporate jargon

## Instagram
- Start with a hook line (first 125 chars matter before "more" cut-off)
- 100–200 words for the caption body
- Conversational, warm, and expressive tone
- End with a call-to-action (save, share, comment, tag a friend)
- Add 8–12 relevant hashtags on a new line at the end

## General rules
- Never use generic filler phrases ("In today's fast-paced world...")
- Be specific — concrete details beat vague statements
- Match the user's brand voice if they describe one
- If no platform is specified, produce all three variants
- Output only the post text, no commentary around it
