When building a content calendar:

## Step 1 — Gather context (ask if not provided)
- What is the brand/topic/niche?
- Which platforms? (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok)
- What time period? (1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month)
- What is the goal? (brand awareness, engagement, sales, followers)
- How many posts per week per platform?

## Step 2 — Content mix (80/20 rule)
- **80% value content**: educational, entertaining, or inspirational
- **20% promotional content**: product/service/offer mentions

## Content pillars to rotate through
1. **Educational** — tips, how-tos, lessons learned
2. **Behind the scenes** — process, team, day-in-the-life
3. **Social proof** — testimonials, results, case studies
4. **Engagement** — questions, polls, challenges
5. **Promotional** — offers, launches, CTAs

## Output format
Present the calendar as a table:

| Date | Platform | Content Pillar | Post Idea | Format |
|------|----------|---------------|-----------|--------|
| Mon 14 | Instagram | Educational | 5 tips for X | Carousel |
| Tue 15 | LinkedIn | Behind the scenes | How we built X | Text post |

## Rules
- Vary content types (text, image, video, carousel, story, reel)
- Space out promotional posts — never two in a row
- Include post ideas specific enough to act on immediately
- Flag ideal posting times per platform if helpful
- After the table, offer to write the actual copy for any post
